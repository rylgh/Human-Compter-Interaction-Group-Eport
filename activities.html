Activities.

A space for the exercises, class activities, discussions, and learning experiences completed throughout the semester.

Term 01

Prelim Activities & Performance Tasks

PT1: Universal Remote Control — Smart Remote

A proposed all-in-one remote for controlling TV entertainment and stove functions. The concept includes multi-device control, voice recognition, child safety features, stove monitoring, adaptive touch controls, and USB-C charging.

View PT1 PDF

Prelim Project: Contactless Card and Payment App

Explains how a BPI Debit Mastercard and GCash work for contactless or app-based payments, including NFC/RFID, payment information, transaction limits, security, and the steps in a payment process.

View Prelim Project PDF

PT2: Innovative Timepiece — Fusion Band

Introduces a smartwatch concept with built-in earbuds for runners. Its proposed features include hydration and heart-rate monitoring, live location sharing, music control, digital wallet and ID access, and solar charging.

View PT2 PDF
Term 02

Midterm Activities

Activity 3: Emotional Interaction — AI Emotion Mirror

Explores the AI Emotion Mirror app, how it estimates facial expressions, tests five emotion categories, and discusses limitations, privacy, fairness, and practical uses.

View Activity 3 PDF

Activity 4: Interfaces — Plants vs. Zombies

Presents three interface enhancements for Plants vs. Zombies: NFC-based tangible plant figures, smart-glasses wearable information, and Smart TV casting, alongside multiplayer and skin-customization visual changes.

View Activity 4 PDF

Midterm Project: Data Gathering — Improving JRU SWIT

Investigates how students view their grades in JRU SWIT and proposes a detailed grade breakdown. The project includes interview, questionnaire, and observation plans, survey findings from 10 respondents, a proposed interface mockup, and a reflection.

View Midterm Project PDF
