03 / Individual reflections

What we learned.

Each member shares important lessons, challenges, realizations, and ways to apply what they learned.

Eunice

One important lesson I learned from Human-Computer Interaction is that technology should be created with the user in mind. An application should not only have useful features; it should also be clear, accessible, and easy to navigate. I found UI/UX design interesting because small choices in layout, colors, labels, and navigation can affect how people use an application. I learned that designing a good interface takes planning, testing, and feedback. In future projects, I want to consider users' needs, keep designs simple and organized, and make applications easier and more convenient to use.

Stacey

Stacey learned that applications should not only function properly but should also be easy to understand and use. Wireframes and interface-layout activities helped her understand UI/UX design. She found it challenging to create simple, organized layouts, but improved through practice, examples, and feedback. She realized that good design focuses on usability, not appearance alone.

Angelyn

Angelyn learned that technology should be designed around the people who use it. Interface-design activities helped her understand usability and user experience. She found it challenging to balance visual appeal and usability, but improved by reviewing lessons, studying app examples, and considering users' needs. She hopes to apply these ideas when creating apps, websites, and systems.

Irene

Irene's submitted reflection focuses on Human Communication. She learned the importance of effective communication, active listening, and understanding others. Group discussions and activities helped her build confidence, express ideas, and work with others. She plans to apply these skills in group projects, studies, her future career, and daily relationships.
